### Fit analysis

**FIT** — with high confidence. Reasons:

1. **Closed label set**: Exactly 10 well-defined categories with descriptions — maps directly to a Choice primitive with 10 criteria.
2. **Semantic task**: Requires understanding ticket intent and matching to a category — precisely what Jev is trained for. No math, date comparison, multi-hop reasoning, or counting.
3. **Benign input distribution**: Insurance support tickets from a fixed dataset (train.tsv / test.tsv). Not a public adversarial surface.
4. **Context size**: Individual tickets are short (1–3 sentences). Well within the 64k/32k limits. No context-rot risk.
5. **No jaggedness concerns**: The 10-category insurance classification has no numeric, temporal, or multi-hop reasoning requirements.
6. **No untrusted-input risk**: Fixed corpus, not a live public API.
